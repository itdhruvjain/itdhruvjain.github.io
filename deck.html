How I work

Four things you can rely on.

I design ahead of the user

What will they actually do, what breaks at the edges, and what requirement is coming in six months that today's schema should already accommodate. It is why Exhibit B's workforce suite modelled shift overlaps, approval chains and award-rule variations before any user hit them.

I write the standards down

Layered backend boundaries, a config-driven reusable front-end layer, SonarQube and ESLint gates — then I mentor people on them. They are what every pull request on the products I lead passes through today.

I own outcomes, not commits

I own client relationships for the products I lead, and I would rather raise an awkward problem early than deliver something on time that quietly does not work. That ownership is what the 2025 Client Excellence Award for the PIQET delivery recognised.
