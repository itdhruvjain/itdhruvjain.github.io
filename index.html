If you hire me

My first month with you.

  1. Week 1

    Learn the business

    What does each system do for real people, and where do they lose time? I start there, before I touch the code.

  2. Week 1–2

    Talk to the people who use it

    I'll sit with the teams who rely on the software and ask why things work the way they do, not just how.

  3. Week 2–3

    Trace real work end to end

    I'll follow a real transaction or report through the code to check my understanding against what actually happens.

  4. Week 3–4

    Ship small, useful things

    I'll take on small tasks early, starting with the parts that are riskiest or break most often, and write down what I learn as I go.
