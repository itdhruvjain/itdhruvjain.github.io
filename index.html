Three tools and a lot of manual work became one platform in two months.

Clinic groups were running rosters in one tool, procedures in another and payroll by hand. I replaced them with one multi-tenant platform that fits how they actually work, and syncs straight to Xero.

Payroll

Each employee entered into Xero one by one, with hand calculations.
