02 · How I'd work with you

My first 30 days.

Something useful finished every week, with a simple number to show it worked. Small changes, written down, and easy to undo.

WEEK 1

Ad reports that build themselves

Ad results from Meta, YouTube and your store in one daily view for each country, with a heads-up when something looks off.

We'll know it worked by: hours of report-building saved
WEEK 2

No more surprise fees

Clear notes about import fees on product and cart pages, a friendly email after purchase, and a costed plan for paying the fees upfront.

We'll know it worked by: fewer fee complaints and reviews
WEEK 3

Support replies, already drafted

Each email gets its order found, checked against your rules and a reply written. A person sends it with one click.

We'll know it worked by: how fast customers hear back
WEEK 4

Happier reviews, fewer lost parcels

Replies ready for low reviews, a weekly note on what customers are saying, and late parcels spotted before anyone has to ask.

We'll know it worked by: reviews answered, fewer "where's my order?" emails
